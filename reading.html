Prior art & sources

  • How to Leak a Secret — Rivest, Shamir & Tauman (2001)
    The paper that introduced ring signatures.
  • Ring signature — Wikipedia
    A general overview and history.
  • Asymmetric-key cryptography in six minutes — Rob Miles
    If digital signatures and public/private keys are new to you.
  • Nostr
    The protocol whose self-held keypairs make a ready-made anonymity set possible.
  • Nostringer
    The original command-line tool for ring signatures over Nostr keys.
  • nostringer-rs
    The Rust scheme this console reimplements in the browser.
  • ZebraSign
    An earlier Mac app for creating and verifying ring signatures.

This is experimental, unaudited software meant for learning and exploration rather than real-world use, and all of its cryptography runs locally in your browser so that no keys ever leave the page.
