OstrichSign

Forge others' signatures alongside your own and let any reader prove that exactly one of the signatures is authentic, but not which one.

Read the introduction → Open the console →

How it works

Enter a message into the console; then sign with your Nostr nsec, your npub, and several others' npubs. Post the resulting signed message somewhere. Readers can then run the verifier to prove that exactly one of those npubs must be an authentic signature. Runs entirely in the browser.

This is experimental, unaudited software meant for learning and exploration rather than real-world use, and all of its cryptography runs locally in your browser so that no keys ever leave the page.
